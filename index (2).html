GOOD TO KNOW

Questions are
welcome here.

Not seeing your question? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

How often should I have a well-woman visit?+

It depends on your age, health history, and needs. A clinician can help you decide what schedule and screenings are appropriate for you.

When should I start cervical cancer screening?+

Recommendations vary by age, medical history, and local guidelines. Your clinician can explain whether Pap testing, HPV testing, or both are appropriate and when to repeat them.

Do I need a referral to make an appointment?+

That depends on your insurance or health system. Contact the clinic before your visit and we'll help clarify the process.

Can I talk about birth control options?+

Absolutely. We can discuss available methods, potential benefits and side effects, and what may fit your health needs and preferences.

What if I'm nervous about my appointment?+

That's completely understandable. Let your care team know how you're feeling. You can ask questions, discuss your options, and speak up if you need a pause.
